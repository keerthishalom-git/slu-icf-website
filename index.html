(01) Who we are

Indian students at SLU who follow Jesus together. Grew up in church or just curious? There's a seat for you at our table.

Faith

Worship, honest Bible study and prayer that carries us through exams, visas and homesickness.

Family

Shared meals, festival celebrations, late-night chai and friends who show up when home is far away.

Service

Welcoming new arrivals, helping each other settle in, and serving our campus and St. Louis.

Rev. Susanna Clive Campus Ministry, Saint Louis University

(02) Our chaplain

Someone in your corner, always.

Our chaplain walks with us through every season of student life. She is present when we need her, cares for us in practical ways, and guides our fellowship in faith.

New to St. Louis? We've been there.
